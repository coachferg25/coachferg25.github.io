Christmas light installation in Austin

If you’re searching for a professional Christmas light installer in Austin, you’re probably picturing one of two things: a clean, classic roofline that makes the whole house look finished, or a full display — entry and windows outlined, trees and shrubs wrapped, the works. Either way, the hard part usually isn’t the idea. It’s finding someone local who will actually design it, climb the ladder, and hang it straight.

That’s where Hung With Care comes in. We connect Austin homeowners with local Christmas light installers who handle rooflines, entries, trees, and full-property displays. Tell us the look you have in mind — warm white, classic color, or a winter mix — and we’ll help start the conversation with an installer in your part of the Austin area. We don’t hang the lights ourselves and we don’t quote prices ourselves; the connected installer provides your quote directly to you. Most installers can quote from your street address using satellite and street images, along with the areas and style you pick.

One thing worth knowing: book early — Austin is one of the highest-demand holiday-lighting markets in the country. Good installers fill up fast, and most Austin installation calendars start filling in September and October. Sending your request early gives you the widest choice of install dates — and one less thing to scramble for in December.

Common questions

Before you book an installer

Straight answers to what Austin homeowners ask most about professional Christmas light installation. Hung With Care does not quote prices itself — the connected installer provides your quote directly to you.

How much does professional Christmas light installation cost in Austin?

It depends on your roofline, how many stories your home has, and the design you choose. Austin full-service installers who publish prices commonly set minimums around $450–$500, and typical full-service jobs run about $800–$3,500. Those are ranges published by other installers, not Hung With Care prices — Hung With Care does not quote prices itself. The installer you’re connected with provides the exact quote directly to you after reviewing your home, often remotely from your street address using satellite and street images plus the areas and style you pick.

When should I book Christmas light installation in Austin?

Reserve in September and October if you can. Installs typically run from late October through early December, and dates fill in the order requests come in, so waiting until after Thanksgiving often means fewer open slots.

Do you install the lights yourselves?

No. Hung With Care does not install lights. We connect Austin homeowners with local Christmas light installers, and the connected installer handles the design, installation, and your quote directly.

Are lights provided, and what about removal and storage?

That varies by installer. Many include commercial-grade lights, in-season service if a bulb goes out, January takedown, and storage until next season, while others install lights you provide. Confirm exactly what’s included in the quote the connected installer gives you.
